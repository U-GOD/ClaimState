# Legal boundaries

ClaimState is software that orders signed evidence among participants. It is not a law firm, a filing office, or a financing counterparty. Nothing in this repository is legal advice.

## Not a lien

A contract write, an HCS message, or an HTS token does not create, attach, or perfect a security interest. ClaimState does not file a UCC financing statement and does not replace one. Priority against persons outside the participating network depends on governing law, notice, and any required filing. Those steps stay outside the kernel.

## Not Article 12 control

The envelope is not control of an electronic record under UCC Article 12. It is not an MLETR title document. It does not transfer legal title to a receivable. Possession of a token, including any later operational receipt, is not an assignment.

## Not MonetaGo

ClaimState is not MonetaGo and does not speak for that registry. A blinded fingerprint committed here is not a MonetaGo duplicate-finance check. Production duplicate detection, if added later, belongs in an adapter. The kernel must not claim that an off-registry pledge is visible.

## The demo advance amount is public

The testnet disbursement moves a demo HTS fungible token. That transfer reveals the advance amount to anyone who can read the network. The leakage is accepted for the demo and is not a privacy claim. Face amount, invoice number, names, bank details, and the commercial PDF stay off the ledger. Production fiat settlement would commit a payment hash. Hedera cannot make an ACH transfer atomic with the reservation.

An HTS receipt, if one is later included in the activation batch, is an operational receipt. It is not an assignment and not a lien. Burning or transferring that receipt does not release the reservation.
